Give me my briefing for today.

1. Call manage_timetable with action "list" to get my classes, and keep only today's.
2. Call manage_tasks with action "list" to get my open tasks; focus on anything due in the next 7 days, overdue items first.
3. Call recall_memory with a query like "my goals and priorities" for one or two things worth reminding me of.

Then write the briefing as if you're saying it out loud: warm, natural sentences, no headings, no bullet lists, no tables, at most about 110 words. Start with a greeting that fits the time of day. Mention today's classes with their times and rooms, then the most urgent deadlines with how many days are left, then one short encouraging line tied to my goals. If something is empty (no classes today, nothing due), say so in a few words instead of skipping it.
